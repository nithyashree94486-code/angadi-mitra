## 2. Proposed & Planned Features

The following features have been designed and documented as part of the Angadi Mitra solution proposal:

- 📋 **Vernacular Voice Assistance:** Speech-to-action interactions in Kannada, Hindi, and other Indian languages.
- 📋 **Image-First Product Dashboard:** Card-based UI showing regional names, high-contrast imagery, and large stock numerals.
- 📋 **Voice-Based Stock Updates:** Natural-language conversational recording of incoming distributor deliveries and manual shelf counts.
- 📋 **Low-Stock & Out-of-Stock Warnings:** Configurable safety thresholds with visual badges and audio alerts.
- 📋 **Expiry Date & Batch Tracking:** Proactive alerts for items approaching shelf-life expiration.
- 📋 **Slow-Moving & Dead-Stock Identification:** Analytics flagging working capital locked in non-moving inventory.
- 📋 **Fast-Moving Velocity Monitoring:** Identification of rapid-sales goods to prevent stockouts.
- 📋 **Agentic Restocking Engine:** Context-aware, budget-constrained purchase recommendations with explicit rationales.
- 📋 **Progressive 7-Day Value Journey:** Guided onboarding delivering initial insights within the first week of use without historical billing records.
- 📋 **Offline-First Data Sync:** Local storage queuing for intermittent village connectivity.
