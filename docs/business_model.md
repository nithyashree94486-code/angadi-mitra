# Business Model — Angadi Mitra

> **Status:** Proposed models — no validation or revenue has been generated.
> **Last updated:** November 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Angadi Mitra targets small and village-level kirana store owners who may have very limited income and are unaccustomed to paying for software. Any business model must therefore be:
- **Low-barrier to adopt** — free to start
- **Priced relative to value** — fees must be small relative to the savings generated
- **Transparent** — no hidden charges
- **Validated** — willingness to pay must be confirmed through field research before assuming

> **Important:** None of the revenue assumptions below have been validated through user research. These are hypotheses to be tested.

---

## 2. Primary Model: Freemium with Paid Tiers

### Free Tier (Always Free)
Designed to get shopkeepers started with zero friction:

| Feature | Free Tier |
|---|---|
| Number of products | Up to 100 |
| Voice stock updates | Yes (limited to 50/month) |
| Low-stock alerts | Yes |
| Basic dashboard | Yes |
| Multilingual interface | Yes |
| Offline mode | Yes |
| Distributor receiving | Yes (up to 5 entries/month) |
| AI restocking suggestions | 1 per month |
| Data retention | 90 days |

**Rationale:** Value must be delivered for free to build trust before asking for payment.

---

### Paid Tier — "Angadi Pro" (~₹99–₹199/month)
Targeted at shops that have experienced value from the free tier:

| Feature | Angadi Pro |
|---|---|
| Unlimited products | Yes |
| Unlimited voice updates | Yes |
| AI restocking suggestions | Unlimited |
| Expiry tracking | Yes |
| Dead-stock analysis | Yes |
| Festival demand estimates | Yes |
| Full stock history | Yes (12 months) |
| Multiple distributors | Yes |
| Priority support | Yes |
| Data export | Yes |

**Price range rationale:**
- ₹99–₹199/month is approximately the cost of a small meal
- If the tool saves ₹1,000–₹2,000/month in expired stock and stockouts, the ROI is clear
- Must be validated through interviews before setting a final price

## 3. Alternative Revenue Streams (Longer-Term, Proposed)

### 3.1 Distributor / Supplier Partnerships (B2B)
Distributors who supply kirana stores could pay to:
- Get aggregate demand signals ("shops in this area are running low on X")
- Offer promotional products through the app
- Receive digital delivery confirmation

> **Important:** This creates a potential conflict of interest — distributor recommendations must never override what is best for the shopkeeper. Shopkeeper interests come first. Any distributor partnership must be clearly disclosed to shopkeepers.

---

### 3.2 Wholesale Ordering Commission (Optional Future Feature)
If online ordering from wholesalers/distributors is integrated, Angadi Mitra could earn a small commission on verified orders placed through the app.

> **Dependency:** Requires verified integration with actual wholesale suppliers. This is proposed as a future feature, not a current integration.

---

### 3.3 JanAI Café / Government Program Partnership (Proposed)
If Angadi Mitra is adopted through the JanAI programme:
- Onboarding assistance provided at JanAI Café centres
- Government or programme subsidy for rural shopkeepers
- Angadi Mitra provides services at reduced or no cost to subsidised users

> **This is a proposed partnership pathway, not a confirmed relationship.**

---

### 3.4 Data Insights (Anonymised, Aggregate, Opt-In)
In the future, anonymised and aggregate demand signals could be valuable to FMCG brands for rural market research. This would only be possible:
- With explicit shopkeeper opt-in
- With full transparency about what data is shared
- With a revenue share passed back to participating shopkeepers
- After legal review of data licensing and privacy regulations

> **This model is highly speculative and would require significant legal and ethical review before implementation.**

## 5. Pricing Assumptions to Validate

Through field research, test:
- [ ] Is ₹99/month acceptable? Or is ₹49/month more appropriate?
- [ ] Do shopkeepers prefer weekly, monthly, or annual billing?
- [ ] Would shopkeepers pay more if the product demonstrably saved them money?
- [ ] Is UPI payment acceptable for subscription billing?
- [ ] Are there shopkeepers who would prefer to pay per-use rather than monthly?

---

## 6. What We Will Not Do

- **No selling of individual shopkeeper data** without explicit consent
- **No advertising in the app** that would distract from core functionality
- **No opaque pricing** — all fees clearly stated before charging
- **No subscription traps** — easy cancellation at any time
- **No partnership that compromises shopkeeper interests** for distributor revenue

---

## 7. Impact Metrics (Proposed)

If the product achieves its goals, we expect to see (to be validated):
- Reduction in monthly expired stock losses
- Reduction in stockout frequency for fast-moving products
- Reduction in dead-stock value as percentage of working capital
- Increase in shopkeeper confidence in purchasing decisions

These metrics would be measured through before/after comparison with consenting shopkeepers in a pilot.

---

*All revenue models and financial projections in this document are hypotheses that have not been validated. No revenue has been generated, and no partnerships have been established.*
