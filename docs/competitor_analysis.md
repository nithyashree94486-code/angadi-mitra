## Angadi Mitra Differentiation

**Existing apps serve a different market:**
- Vyapar, myBillBook — designed for businesses with GST billing needs and existing records
- GoFrugal, Marg ERP — designed for established retailers and distributors with IT infrastructure
- Khatabook — focused on credit management (udhaar), not inventory intelligence
- BharatPe — focused on payments, not inventory management

**None of them combine:**
1. ✅ Genuine agentic AI workflow (function calling, multi-step planning)
2. ✅ Village-first and voice-first design
3. ✅ 8+ Indian language support with verified voice I/O
4. ✅ Budget-aware AI recommendations with mandatory shopkeeper approval
5. ✅ Image-first UX for first-time digital users
6. ✅ 7-day onboarding from zero data
7. ✅ Explicit offline-first architecture

**Angadi Mitra's differentiation** is not that inventory management itself is new — it's the combination of genuine agentic intelligence, accessibility (voice, image, language), and a design philosophy built specifically around India's smallest kirana stores.
